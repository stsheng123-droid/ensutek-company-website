QUALITY ASSURANCE

QUALITY CONTROL

Quality control covers incoming materials, key production stages and finished products.

Dedicated in-house equipment supports dimensional, functional and environmental verification.

01

Incoming & Dimensional Inspection

Critical dimensions, material condition and first-piece samples are checked before production. CMM and optical measurement equipment support accurate verification.

02

Functional & Performance Verification

Gas spring force, leakage and operating performance are checked using dedicated fixtures to support stable product performance before final assembly and shipment.

03

Reliability & Environmental Testing

Salt spray, thermal shock, temperature and humidity tests evaluate coating durability and product reliability under different operating and transportation conditions.
